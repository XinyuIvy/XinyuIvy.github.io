I am originally from northern China. Outside of research, I enjoy listening to music, singing, and staying active.

I develop and apply statistical methods to answer complex questions in biomedical and scientific research.

My work spans neuroimaging, clinical-trial endpoint development, longitudinal and recurrent-event analysis, and reproducible scientific computing. I am especially interested in methods that make complex data more interpretable and useful in practice.

Before joining Vanderbilt, I earned an MS in Biostatistics from Yale University and a BS in Statistics from Southwestern University of Finance and Economics.
