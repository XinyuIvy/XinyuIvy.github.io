AI Usage Dashboard — Cross-Device AI Quota Observability ↗

I built a privacy-aware dashboard that consolidates quota usage, reset windows, and account status across multiple AI coding services. A Python REST service normalizes provider data for a responsive web dashboard, macOS menu-bar display, and iPhone widget, while caching, last-known-good fallbacks, and stale-state labels preserve useful status during partial failures. Credentials remain on the authenticated Mac, with read-only quota information delivered privately across devices.

Ivy Job Radar — Verified Job Intelligence and Application Tracking ↗

I am developing a full-stack job-intelligence and application-tracking system that collects opportunities from job boards, public APIs, company pages, and ATS feeds, then reconciles them against employer evidence. The pipeline canonicalizes and deduplicates records, applies explainable degree, experience, sponsorship, seniority, region, and role-family constraints, and monitors source health and data quality. A React and TypeScript interface with server-side persistence supports job review and the broader application workflow.
